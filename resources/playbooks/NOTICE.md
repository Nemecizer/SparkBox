Reproduce the bundle with scripts/playbooks/import-library.py and the reviewed
cache described in scripts/playbooks/README.md. The importer verifies
every source hash and does not execute downloaded material. Images, notebooks,
installers and other executable assets are linked at the pinned source revision;
they are not included in this documentation bundle.
